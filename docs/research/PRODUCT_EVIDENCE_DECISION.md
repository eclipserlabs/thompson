# Product Evidence Decision (Phase 8)

## Thresholds (pre-committed logic, applied to measured results)
- Pursue adaptive execution only with a rankable, replicated net win over
  the competent static after ALL operating costs, at the pre-registered
  quality floor.
- Concentrate on evidence/evaluation if adaptation ties but the
  measurement apparatus proves independently valuable.
- Stop runtime expansion if statics match within the economic margin.

## Application
- No rankable T3-over-T1 win in 8 eval scenarios; ties where static is
  optimal; material losses under missingness/tails/traps; universal ties
  under drift. Threshold 1: NOT MET.
- Evidence value: ledgers + verification contract + refusal gates caught
  real defects during this program (phantom decisions, hash brittleness,
  unflocked safety log) and exposed every planted regression. Threshold 2:
  MET — the evaluation library justifies itself with or without adaptive
  selection.
- Contextual routing: task-heterogeneity headroom unmeasured (difficulty
  scenarios show aggregate confounding but no contextual policy exists to
  test). No evidence either way: do not build yet.

## Recommendation
**Concentrate on the evidence and evaluation library; pause adaptive-
execution expansion pending representative external workloads.** The next
experiment, if any: de-identified partner workloads through the documented
interface, powered (longer horizons, replicated seeds) for the
pre-registered margin — with the explicit stop rule that a second null
result shelves the adaptive product while retaining the evidence stack.

## Cost attribution (where spending goes, measured)
Per settled job: persistence ~5–6ms wall / ~500B–1.8KB bytes dominate;
policy math ~50–700ns; verification/human/review costs are workload truth
(synthetic here); decision-system overhead is the evidence bytes above.
Inference/retry/fallback/human shares are scenario-dependent by construction.

## Pilot-partner minimums
Frozen safety envelope, prequalified arms + designated fallback, verifier
independence with provenance, per-arm cost metering (or explicit nulls
with budgets), maturation windows, several hundred matured jobs per arm
minimum (n=160 total left CIs too wide to rank anything).

## Gating for bigger bets
Contextual routing: needs measured heterogeneity headroom first.
Multi-replica: needs evidence single-writer is insufficient. Ledger
lifecycle: needs retention requirements + the >10k projection decision.
